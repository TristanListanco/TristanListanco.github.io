About
Tristan

I study Computer Applications, majoring in Embedded Systems, at Mindanao State University – Iligan Institute of Technology. My work runs from firmware on STM32 boards to iOS and watchOS apps to YOLO models on a Raspberry Pi in a pepper field.

I'm the lead student member of a university research project on IoT-based aphid detection for bell pepper farms in Northern Mindanao. I'm main author of a systematic review accepted at EUSPN 2026 in Almaty and co-author of a paper published by Springer in the ICICT 2026 proceedings.

Outside the lab I serve as College Vice Governor of the CCS Student Council, work as the Philippine focal person for UNESCO SeaBRNet Youth, and edit video. I founded AquaGuard, an IoT water-monitoring project funded through UNESCO Asia.

Pepper Watch

Research build · 2026

Aphid damage detection for bell pepper farms, built for the research project I help lead. One version runs entirely on an iPhone or iPad, with an Apple Watch app and widgets for field status. The other runs YOLOv8n on a Raspberry Pi 5 above the crop canopy and serves a live dashboard over its own Wi-Fi network, so a farmer only has to join the network to see results.
